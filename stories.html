How We've Helped

CASE — 01

Building a finance function for a professional football club, from scratch

Situation

A client preparing to operate a professional football club franchise needed a robust financial system from day one — covering ticketing and sponsorship revenue, player and staff costs, and statutory compliance.

What we did

Built the accounting framework from the ground up: chart of accounts, bookkeeping, GST accounting and filing, payroll, bank reconciliations and monthly MIS reporting for management.

Outcome

Accofin became the club's ongoing finance and accounting function, giving management financial visibility and statutory compliance from day one of operations.

CASE — 02

Labour law due diligence ahead of an acquisition

Situation

A client evaluating the acquisition of an Indian company needed to understand the target's compliance position and potential liabilities around employee benefits before proceeding.

What we did

Reviewed EPF, ESI, bonus and gratuity records, reconciled statutory contributions against payroll, and flagged delayed or non-compliant obligations.

Outcome

The client entered the transaction with a clear picture of employee-related compliance risk, informing their final acquisition decision.
