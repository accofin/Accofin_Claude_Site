What We Handle

Accounting, GST, TDS & Compliance Services

Outsourced finance and compliance support for global companies operating in India and growth-stage Indian businesses — from day-to-day bookkeeping to statutory filings.

Core services

Register of Engagements
ACC — 01

Accounting & Bookkeeping Coordination

Keeping your day-to-day financial records accurate — every payment, invoice and expense logged and matched, your bank accounts reconciled, and your books closed and summarised every month, so you always know exactly where your money stands.

GST — 02

GST Compliance Support

GST (Goods and Services Tax) is the tax India charges on the sale of goods and services, similar to VAT elsewhere. We gather your sales and purchase data, check it for errors, and manage the monthly filings so your GST returns go in correctly and on time.

TDS — 03

TDS / Withholding Coordination

TDS (Tax Deducted at Source) is India's system of deducting tax upfront on payments such as salaries, rent, contractor fees and professional services, and depositing it with the government. We work out the correct amount on each payment, keep vendor records in order, and prepare the periodic TDS returns.

PAY — 04

Payroll & Labour Compliance Coordination

Beyond paying salaries, Indian employers must also contribute to employee welfare schemes — EPF (a retirement savings fund), ESI (health insurance for eligible staff), professional tax and local labour registrations. We calculate these every month and keep the filings current, so your team is covered and you stay compliant.

SET — 05

India Subsidiary / Business Setup Support

Whether you're a foreign company opening your first India office, or a founder registering a new company, LLP or firm, we handle the incorporation paperwork and statutory registrations, and set up a compliance calendar from day one so nothing gets missed later.

MIS — 06

Management Reporting & MIS Packs

Regular, easy-to-read reports on how your business is actually performing — monthly summaries, key numbers and cash flow snapshots — built for founders and head-office teams who need clarity without digging through raw accounting data.

DD — 07

Due Diligence & Transaction Support

Before an acquisition, investment or major decision, due diligence is a thorough check of a company's finances, tax position and compliance history to confirm there are no hidden risks. We put together structured reports covering exactly that, so you can decide with the full picture in hand.
