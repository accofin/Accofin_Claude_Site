About Accofin Consultancy

Founded in 2021 in Trivandrum, Kerala — an outsourced finance and compliance execution partner for businesses in India and abroad.

Accofin operates as a finance and compliance execution partner built for India's regulatory environment — not a transactional filing service, but an operational layer that maps obligations, builds compliance calendars, coordinates inputs and maintains documentation discipline.

The solution is not just expertise — it is process discipline: a predictable operating system, strong coordination and confidentiality-first workflows, turning intricate requirements into a manageable execution system for global companies expanding into India and growth-stage Indian businesses alike.

“The solution is not just expertise — it is process discipline.”

Confidentiality & trust

A privacy-first working model: wherever possible, your documents stay on your own systems. Where soft copies need sharing, we work from a copy in your own drive with view-only access for our team — so you keep control and a log of exactly when we've accessed it.
