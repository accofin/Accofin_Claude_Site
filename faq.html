Before You Reach Out

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on timelines, pricing, data security and how to get started.

How long does setting up an India subsidiary take?

Typically two to six weeks. The first week covers document collection, name reservation and verifying your paperwork; the remaining time is the Ministry of Corporate Affairs' own processing and query turnaround.

How long does it take to onboard as an ongoing accounting or compliance client?

Usually two to four weeks — the first week to review your existing processes, and the rest to clear any backlog. It can take longer if there's more than a year of backlog data to work through.

How is pricing structured?

Case by case, as either a retainer or a project-based fee. Every business's scope is different enough that we don't quote a blanket rate — we price after understanding what you actually need.

How do you handle our financial documents and data?

Wherever possible, documents stay on your own systems. Where soft copies need sharing, we work from a copy in your own drive with view-only access for our team — so you keep control and a log of exactly when we've accessed it.

What do we need to prepare before the first conversation?

Nothing in particular. We start by listening to what you need and shape our approach around it, rather than running everyone through the same fixed checklist.
